Coastal Funding Corporation — DSCR is the DSCR loan blog of Coastal Funding Corporation (NMLS 103035), a mortgage broker. It covers debt service coverage ratio (DSCR) loans for real estate investors: financing that qualifies on a rental property's income rather than the borrower's personal income. Coastal Funding Corporation offers DSCR investment-property loans nationwide.
