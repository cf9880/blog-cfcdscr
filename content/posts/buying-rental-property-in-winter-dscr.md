**Yes, buying rental property in winter can work in an investor's favor. Fewer buyers shop over the holidays, homes take longer to sell, and many sellers want a deal done by year-end. NAR's median days on market rose to 39 in December 2025, from 35 a year earlier. The trade-off: rents and leasing activity usually soften in winter, so underwrite carefully.**

Here's what the data says, how rent seasonality affects your DSCR, what to know about year-end tax timing, and how to close a DSCR loan before Dec. 31.

## Key Takeaways

- **Seller leverage:** Redfin estimated U.S. sellers outnumbered buyers by **45%** in December 2025, the widest gap in its records back to 2013.
- **More supply now:** U.S. inventory reached **1.60 million** homes in September 2026, a **4.8-month supply** ([NAR](https://www.nar.realtor/newsroom/existing-home-sales-increased-1-5-in-september-according-to-nar-report)).
- **Winter rents soften:** Apartment List's national median rent fell **0.8%** in December 2025.
- **Tax timing is not automatic:** Depreciation starts when a rental is *ready and available for rent*, not on the closing date. Talk to a tax pro.
- **Start the DSCR loan early:** Appraisal, rent schedule, insurance, and title all slow down around the holidays.

## Why Can Winter Be a Good Time to Buy Investment Property?

Investors buy on numbers, and winter often improves the numbers on the purchase side:

- **Slower sales.** NAR's median days on market was **39 in December 2025** versus 35 a year earlier ([NAR REALTORS® Confidence Index](https://cms.nar.realtor/sites/default/files/2026-01/2025-12-realtors-confidence-index-01-21-2026.pdf)).
- **More sellers than buyers.** Redfin estimated a **45%** seller surplus in December 2025 ([Redfin](https://www.redfin.com/news/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Buyers-vs-Sellers-Release-Jan-2026.pdf)).
- **Price cuts.** Realtor.com found **12.9%** of listings had a price reduction in December 2025 ([Realtor.com](https://www.realtor.com/research/december-2025-data/)).
- **Flat prices.** NAR's national median was **$405,400** in December 2025, up just 0.4% from a year earlier.

Heading into this winter, supply is higher still. NAR reported a **4.8-month supply** in September 2026, up from 4.5 months a year earlier, and a median price of **$417,500** (+1.3%).

### Who sells in December?

Freddie Mac notes that winter sellers are often **motivated to negotiate** ([Freddie Mac My Home](https://myhome.freddiemac.com/blog/homebuying/buy-or-sell-in-winter)). For investors, that can include:

- **Tired landlords** who don't want another lease renewal or another year of repairs
- **Relocating owners** who need to sell on a deadline
- **Listings that went stale** after the summer and fall seasons
- **Sellers with year-end goals** of their own

Ask for terms that help cash flow, such as **seller-paid closing costs**, a **rate buydown**, or **repair credits** for items like HVAC or a roof.

## How Does Rent Seasonality Affect a Winter Purchase?

Rent is the "D" side of your DSCR, so timing matters.

- **Rents dip in the off-season.** Apartment List reported the national median rent fell **0.8%** month over month in December 2025, its fifth straight monthly decline.
- **Peak growth has moved earlier.** Since 2023, Apartment List says national rent growth has peaked in **March**, not May, and turned negative by **August** ([Apartment List](https://www.apartmentlist.com/research/the-rental-markets-peak-season-is-becoming-less-pronounced)).
- **Today's market is cool but firming.** As of September 2026, the national median rent was **$1,388**, multifamily vacancy was **7.0%** (down from a 7.3% peak in February), and units took **34 days** to lease ([Apartment List](https://www.apartmentlist.com/research/national-rent-data)).

**What that means for you:**

1. **A vacant property may sit longer.** Budget for one to two months of vacancy if you buy an empty home in December.
2. **A home with a tenant in place** can avoid winter leasing risk. Review the lease, rent roll, and deposit before closing.
3. **Plan lease end dates for spring.** A lease that ends in March through June usually hits a busier leasing market.

## How Does Winter Rent Change Your DSCR?

**DSCR** (debt service coverage ratio) is the property's monthly rent divided by its monthly payment, called **PITIA**: principal, interest, taxes, insurance, and any HOA dues.

Many lenders use an existing lease or the appraiser's market rent estimate. Here is a simple example with a $1,800 PITIA (illustration only):

| Rent used by lender | PITIA | DSCR |
|---|---|---|
| $2,200 | $1,800 | 1.22 |
| $2,100 | $1,800 | 1.17 |
| $1,950 | $1,800 | 1.08 |

A $250 swing in rent can move your ratio enough to change your pricing or down payment. Underwrite with **conservative** rent. Our guide on [how to calculate DSCR](/posts/how-to-calculate-dscr/) walks through the math.

Rates feed the other half of the ratio. Mortgage News Daily's 30-year fixed index was **7.33%** on Oct. 26, 2026, and Freddie Mac's weekly average was **7.32%** on Oct. 22, versus 6.19% a year earlier ([Freddie Mac](https://www.freddiemac.com/pmms)). Those are conventional benchmarks, not DSCR rates, but they show the direction of the market. See our [DSCR rates update](/posts/dscr-loan-rates-investors-2026/) for more.

## What Year-End Tax Timing Should Investors Know?

This is general information, not tax advice. Here is what IRS Publication 527 says about residential rentals ([IRS](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p527)):

- **Depreciation starts when the property is "placed in service,"** meaning ready and available for rent, even if no tenant has moved in yet. Closing alone doesn't start it.
- **Residential rental buildings** are depreciated over **27.5 years** using a **mid-month convention**. A rental placed in service in December gets only about half a month of depreciation for that year.
- **Land is not depreciable.** Only the building portion of your price counts.
- **Expenses** for managing and maintaining the rental are generally deductible from the time it is available for rent.
- **Points on a rental loan** are generally deducted over the life of the loan, not all in the year you pay them.
- **Many closing costs**, such as title insurance, recording fees, and transfer taxes, are added to your basis instead of deducted right away.

**Bottom line:** A December closing can still make sense, but don't buy just for a tax deduction. Have your CPA run the numbers first.

## How Do You Close a DSCR Loan Before the End of the Year?

NAR reported a **median of 30 days to close** in December 2025. Give yourself more room on an investment purchase:

1. **Get pre-qualified now.** DSCR loans usually don't need tax returns or W-2s, but lenders still check credit, assets, and reserves.
2. **Order the appraisal early.** Ask for a **market rent schedule** with it. Appraisers get busy before the holidays.
3. **Line up insurance.** You'll need a landlord (dwelling) policy, plus flood coverage if required.
4. **Have reserves ready.** Lenders commonly want several months of PITIA in the bank.
5. **Watch the calendar.** Thanksgiving is Nov. 26, Christmas is Friday, Dec. 25, and New Year's Day is Friday, Jan. 1, 2027. Title companies and county recorders may close or slow down.
6. **Collect lease documents** if the property has a tenant: the signed lease, deposit records, and an estoppel letter if your lender asks for one.

These are **industry ranges**, not Coastal Funding program terms. Each lender sets its own guidelines:

- **Credit score:** about **620 to 700+**, with better leverage at higher scores
- **Down payment:** commonly **20% to 25%** on a purchase
- **DSCR:** **1.0 or higher** is a common target; some programs go lower with tighter terms
- **Reserves:** often **3 to 6 months** of PITIA
