## Key Takeaways

- **DSCR = monthly rent ÷ monthly PITIA.** A ratio of 1.0 means the rent covers the payment.
- **Typical industry ranges** include a credit score of about 620 to 700+, 20% to 25% down, a DSCR near 1.0 or higher, and several months of reserves. Exact rules vary by lender.
- **Entity loans are common.** Many programs allow an LLC, usually with a personal guarantee.
- **Property types** often include single-family homes, townhomes, 2- to 4-unit buildings, and many condos.

## What Problem Does a DSCR Loan Solve?

Conventional investment loans still look at your personal debt-to-income ratio. That can be a poor fit if you are self-employed, if depreciation reduces your taxable income, or if you already have several mortgages.

A DSCR loan changes the main question to: **Does this property pay for itself?** Our [how to calculate DSCR](/posts/how-to-calculate-dscr/) guide walks through the formula. PITIA includes principal, interest, taxes, insurance, and association dues, so insurance and HOA costs are not optional details.

These loans are generally non-QM. Lenders write their own guidelines, which is why comparing programs matters.
