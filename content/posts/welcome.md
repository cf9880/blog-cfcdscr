Welcome to the Coastal Funding DSCR Blog. This blog is all about **DSCR loans**: investment-property mortgages that qualify mainly on a property's rental income instead of your personal income.

## What you'll find here

- **DSCR basics:** what a debt service coverage ratio is and how lenders use it.
- **The math:** DSCR = gross monthly rent ÷ monthly PITIA (principal, interest, taxes, insurance and HOA dues). A ratio of 1.0 or higher means the rent covers the payment.
- **Investor scenarios:** long-term rentals, short-term rentals, and buying in an LLC.
- **Process tips:** what to gather before you apply and what to expect.
