**Yes, a first-time investor can often get a DSCR loan. A DSCR (debt service coverage ratio) loan qualifies you based on a rental property's income rather than your personal income, so you don't need tax returns or pay stubs. New investors may face stricter terms, such as a larger down payment or more reserves, depending on the lender.**

If buying your first rental is on your 2027 goals list, the weeks around Thanksgiving and the new year are a good time to prepare. Here's how DSCR loans work for beginners, what lenders look for, and what the latest rent data says.

## Key Takeaways

- **DSCR = rent ÷ payment.** A ratio of 1.0 or higher is a common target.
- **No personal income documents** are usually required, but credit, assets and reserves still matter.
- **First-time investors** may need more down payment or reserves with some lenders.
- **Rents are flat nationally:** $1,692 median asking rent in October 2026, up $1 year over year (Realtor.com).
- **Investment only.** DSCR loans are for non-owner-occupied rentals.

## What Is a DSCR Loan?

A **DSCR loan** is a business-purpose mortgage for rental property. Instead of looking at your W-2s, the lender compares the property's rent with its full monthly payment, called **PITIA** (principal, interest, taxes, insurance and association dues).

**DSCR = monthly rent ÷ monthly PITIA**

- **Above 1.0:** rent more than covers the payment.
- **1.0:** rent covers it exactly.
- **Below 1.0:** rent falls short. Some lenders allow this with tighter terms.

Our step-by-step guide on [how to calculate DSCR](/posts/how-to-calculate-dscr/) includes more examples.

## Can You Get a DSCR Loan With No Rental Experience?

Often, yes. Lender policies vary, but first-time investors commonly see some of these differences:

- **Lower maximum loan-to-value**, which means a bigger down payment
- **Higher minimum credit scores**
- **More months of reserves** in the bank after closing
- **Limits on certain property types**, such as short-term rentals

Many DSCR programs start with credit scores around 620 to 660 and down payments of 20% to 25%, as our [DSCR loan requirements guide](/posts/dscr-loan-requirements/) explains. ## A Simple First-Rental Example

This is a **hypothetical** example to show the math, not a quote:

- Purchase price: $300,000
- Down payment: 25% ($75,000), loan of $225,000
- Market rent from the appraiser's rent schedule: $2,300 a month
- Estimated PITIA: $2,050 a month

DSCR = $2,300 ÷ $2,050 = **1.12**. Rent covers the payment with some room to spare, which many lenders look for. The appraiser's **rent schedule** (often Fannie Mae Form 1007) sets the rent the lender will use; see our post on the [DSCR appraisal rent schedule](/posts/dscr-appraisal-rent-schedule-form-1007/).

What it means for a first-time investor: national rent growth is slow and vacancy is higher, so be conservative. Underwrite your deal with realistic rent, a vacancy cushion and current insurance quotes. Local markets can differ a lot from national averages.

## What About Interest Rates?

DSCR rates are set by each lender and are usually higher than owner-occupied loan rates. For context, Freddie Mac's 30-year average for owner-occupied conventional loans was 7.11% on November 12, 2026, and Mortgage News Daily's daily index was 7.46% on November 18, 2026. Your DSCR rate will depend on your credit, down payment, DSCR, loan size and any prepayment penalty option.

## How to Prepare for Your First DSCR Loan

1. **Check your credit** and pay down revolving balances where you can.
2. **Save for down payment, closing costs and reserves.** Reserves are often measured in months of PITIA.
3. **Decide how you'll hold title.** Many investors buy in an LLC; see our guide to [DSCR loans for LLCs](/posts/dscr-loan-llc/).
4. **Pick a rental strategy**, such as long-term, mid-term or short-term, since it affects how rent is counted.
5. **Get prequalified** before you make offers, so you know what DSCR a deal needs.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can a first-time investor get a DSCR loan?

Often, yes. Many DSCR lenders work with first-time investors, though some ask for a higher credit score, a larger down payment or more reserves when you have no rental history.

### Do I need landlord experience for a DSCR loan?

Not always. Experience requirements vary by lender. Some programs require none, while others limit loan-to-value or property types, such as short-term rentals, for investors without a track record.

### How is DSCR calculated?

DSCR equals the property's monthly rent divided by its monthly housing payment, including principal, interest, taxes, insurance and any HOA dues. A DSCR of 1.0 means rent covers the payment exactly.

### Can I use a DSCR loan to buy my own home?

No. DSCR loans are business-purpose loans for investment properties you will not live in. Primary residences need a different loan type.

### Are rents rising in 2026?

Nationally, barely. Realtor.com reported a $1,692 median asking rent in the 50 largest metros in October 2026, up $1 from a year earlier, the first annual increase since June 2023. Local rents vary widely.
