- **There's no single "best" DSCR lender.** The right fit depends on your DSCR, credit, leverage, and property type.
- **Compare apples to apples:** same loan amount, LTV, prepay term, and lock period.
- **Prepayment penalties matter** as much as the rate if you might sell or refinance.
- **Benchmark:** Freddie Mac's 30-year conventional average was **7.32%** on Oct. 22, 2026. DSCR loans are priced differently.
- **Investors are active:** NAR says investors and second-home buyers were **20%** of September 2026 existing-home sales, up from 15%.

## What makes the best DSCR lender?

DSCR (debt service coverage ratio) loans qualify you based on the property's rent compared with its PITIA (principal, interest, taxes, insurance, and association dues), not your personal income. Because these are non-QM loans, every lender writes its own rules. Judge each one on:

1. **DSCR minimum:** Is it 1.0, 1.25, or are there sub-1.0 options?
2. **Leverage:** maximum loan-to-value (LTV) for purchase and cash-out.
3. **Total cost:** rate, points, and lender fees.
4. **Prepayment options:** for example, a 5-4-3-2-1 step-down, 3-year, or no prepay.
5. **Property types:** single-family, 2-4 units, condos, short-term rentals, small multifamily.
6. **Vesting:** closing in an LLC.
7. **Speed and communication:** clear conditions and a realistic timeline.

## How to choose a DSCR lender: investor checklist

1. **Calculate your DSCR** before you shop: monthly rent ÷ PITIA. See [How to Calculate DSCR](/posts/how-to-calculate-dscr/).
2. **Decide your hold period.** It drives the right prepayment structure.
3. **Request three quotes the same day** with identical loan amount, LTV, prepay term, and lock period.
4. **Ask how rent is determined:** lease, appraiser's market rent (Form 1007), or STR history.
5. **Confirm reserves and seasoning** requirements, especially for cash-out.
6. **Verify licensing** on [NMLS Consumer Access](https://www.nmlsconsumeraccess.org/).
7. **Ask for a written fee sheet** and compare every line.

### Questions to ask a DSCR lender

- What's your minimum DSCR, and how does pricing change at 1.0, 1.15, and 1.25?
- What's the maximum LTV for my purchase or cash-out?
- What prepayment options do you offer, and what does each do to the rate?
- How do you calculate short-term rental income?
- Can I close in my LLC, and do you require a personal guarantee?
- How many months of reserves do you require?
- What's a realistic closing timeline from application?

## DSCR lender vs. DSCR broker

A **direct lender** can only offer its own programs. A **DSCR broker** compares programs from multiple wholesale lenders, which helps when your deal doesn't fit a typical box: a DSCR under 1.0, a short-term rental, a condo, or a portfolio of properties.
