**There is still time to close on a rental property before year-end 2026 if you start a DSCR preapproval now and write a contract with a realistic late-December closing date. A 2026 Intercontinental Exchange study cited by Zillow found that the average purchase closing takes about 37 days, and many financed purchases close 30 to 45 days after the contract is signed. Closing by Thanksgiving is generally not realistic unless you are already under contract.**

Investors sometimes want a 2026 closing for tax planning, a 1031 deadline, or so a tenant can move in at the first of the year. Those are fair goals. They only work if the loan file, the entity, and the rent documentation are started in time. This is a planning guide, not a promise that any date will be met, and it is not tax advice.

## How Many Days Are Left for a Rental Close?

From mid-November:

- **Thanksgiving, November 26:** about 11 days, shorter than a typical closing.
- **Christmas, December 25:** about 40 days, possible but tight once holiday closures are included.
- **December 31:** about 46 days, a more comfortable window if the appraisal, title work, and entity documents stay on schedule.

[Zillow](https://www.zillow.com/learn/how-long-does-it-take-to-close-on-a-house/), citing a 2026 ICE study, reports an average close of about 37 days. That figure is a national purchase average, not a DSCR guarantee. A delayed [Form 1007 rent schedule](/posts/dscr-appraisal-rent-schedule-form-1007/), a short-term rental income review, or missing LLC paperwork can add days.

If you are already under contract, ask whether the current date still works. If you are still shopping, treat year-end as possible, not automatic. Our [guide to buying a rental in winter](/posts/buying-rental-property-in-winter-dscr/) covers market-season issues; this post is about the calendar.

## What Year-End Issues Are Unique to Investors?

**1031 exchanges.** Identification and closing deadlines are statutory. If you are already in an exchange, read our [DSCR and 1031 year-end guide](/posts/dscr-loan-1031-exchange-year-end/) and tell us the dates at the first call. A new loan application with two weeks left is a poor plan.

**Entity documents.** If the loan will be in an LLC, the entity should be in good standing, with the operating agreement and ownership details ready, before the last week of December. See our [DSCR loan for an LLC](/posts/dscr-loan-llc/) post.

**Tax year and placed-in-service questions.** Closing in 2026 versus 2027 can matter for your tax return, but the rules depend on when the property is placed in service and on your overall situation. We will not tell you that a December 31 closing automatically creates a 2026 tax benefit. Ask a CPA or tax attorney.

**Tenant timing.** Closing in late December does not guarantee a January 1 tenant. If occupancy is part of your plan, build time for make-ready work and leasing after you have the keys.

**The Closing Disclosure wait.** If the loan is covered by the same timing rules as other closed-end mortgages, you must receive the Closing Disclosure at least [three business days before closing](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/when-do-i-get-a-closing-disclosure-en-179/). Thanksgiving Day and Christmas Day do not count as business days. Some business-purpose DSCR loans are treated differently; your loan officer will tell you what applies to your program. Do not assume you can sign on the last business hour before a holiday.
