**In most DSCR loans, the appraiser's rent schedule sets the rent the lender uses to calculate your ratio. For a one-unit rental, that's usually Fannie Mae's Form 1007, the Single-Family Comparable Rent Schedule. For two to four units, it's the rent section of Form 1025. If the market rent comes in low, your DSCR, loan amount, or rate can change.**

Investors often focus on the purchase price and the rate. But on a DSCR loan, the rent number on the appraisal can matter just as much. Here's how the DSCR appraisal rent schedule works and how to prepare for it.

## Key Takeaways

- **DSCR = rent ÷ PITIA.** PITIA is principal, interest, taxes, insurance, and association dues.
- **Form 1007** estimates market rent for a one-unit property using comparable rentals.
- **Form 1025** is the appraisal report for two- to four-unit properties and includes the rents for each unit.
- **Lease vs. market rent:** Many DSCR lenders use the lower of the two, or allow a higher lease with proof it's being paid. Rules vary by lender.
- **A low rent estimate can be challenged** with better comparable rentals, but there's no guarantee it will change.

## What Is a DSCR Appraisal Rent Schedule?

A **rent schedule** is the part of an appraisal where the appraiser estimates what the property should rent for in today's market. Fannie Mae's [appraisal forms and exhibits](https://guide-selling.fanniemae.com/sel/b4-1.2-01/appraisal-report-forms-and-exhibits) list Form 1007 for one-unit properties when rental income is used to qualify, and Form 1025 for two- to four-unit properties.

DSCR loans aren't Fannie Mae loans, but many DSCR lenders use these same familiar forms because appraisers know them well.

## How Does Form 1007 Work?

Fannie Mae's Form 1007 says it's "intended to provide the appraiser with a familiar format to estimate the market rent of the subject property." In practice, the appraiser:

1. **Picks comparable rentals.** The form has space for three comparables, with their addresses, distance from your property, lease dates, and monthly rent.
2. **Adjusts for differences.** The form says adjustments should be made only for items of significant difference, such as utilities included, furniture, size, bedrooms, condition, or amenities.
3. **Reconciles to a market rent.** The appraiser gives a final opinion of monthly market rent for your property.

If the property is already rented, the form also records the current lease rent so the lender can compare it with market rent.

## How Does Form 1025 Work for 2-4 Units?

For a duplex, triplex, or fourplex, the appraisal uses Form 1025, the Small Residential Income Property Appraisal Report. It includes current rents and the appraiser's market rent opinion for each unit. Your DSCR uses the total rent for all units. Our [DSCR guide to 2-4 unit properties](/posts/dscr-loan-2-4-unit-multifamily/) covers multifamily rules in more detail.

## Lease Rent or Market Rent: Which Does the Lender Use?

It depends on the lender and the situation. Common approaches in the industry include:

- **Vacant or new purchase:** The appraiser's market rent is used.
- **Leased property:** The lender uses the lower of the lease or market rent.
- **Lease above market:** Some lenders allow the higher lease rent if you can document it with a signed lease and recent proof of payment, sometimes with a cap.
- **Short-term rentals:** Some lenders use 12 months of booking history or a third-party short-term rental income estimate instead of a long-term rent schedule. See our [DSCR guide for Airbnb rentals](/posts/dscr-loan-short-term-rental/).

These are typical practices as of November 2026, not Coastal Funding program terms. Always confirm which rent your lender will use before you lock.

## Why Does the Rent Number Matter So Much?

Here's an illustrative example. It is not a quote:

- Purchase price: $300,000 with 25% down
- Loan: $225,000, 30-year fixed at a hypothetical 7.75%
- Principal and interest: about $1,612
- Taxes $300 and insurance $150 per month
- **Total PITIA: about $2,062**

| Rent used by lender | DSCR |
|---|---|
| Appraiser market rent of $2,100 | About 1.02 |
| Documented lease of $2,300 | About 1.12 |
| Low market rent of $1,900 | About 0.92 |

A swing of $200 in rent moves the ratio by about 0.10. Many lenders price better at 1.00 or 1.25 and above, and some allow ratios below 1.00 with more down or higher pricing. To run your own numbers, see [how to calculate DSCR](/posts/how-to-calculate-dscr/).

## How Can You Prepare for the DSCR Appraisal?

1. **Research rents first.** Check active listings and recent leases for similar homes nearby, matching beds, baths, size, and condition.
2. **Share a signed lease and payment proof** if the home is rented.
3. **Make the property show well.** Finish repairs before the inspection. Condition affects both value and rent.
4. **List upgrades.** A short list of recent improvements, like a new roof, HVAC, or appliances, helps the appraiser.
5. **Note what's included.** If you pay utilities or include furniture, say so, since the form adjusts for those items.
6. **Build a cushion.** Underwrite your deal at a slightly lower rent so a conservative estimate doesn't break it.

## What If the Appraisal Rent Comes in Low?

You have options:

- **Request a reconsideration of value.** Through your lender, submit better comparable rentals the appraiser may have missed. There's no guarantee of a change.
- **Increase your down payment** to lower PITIA and raise the ratio.
- **Consider an interest-only option or a rate buydown,** if your lender calculates DSCR on the lower payment.
- **Look at a lender that allows a lower DSCR,** often with more down or higher pricing.
- **Renegotiate the price** if the rent shows the deal doesn't work as planned.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is a rent schedule on a DSCR appraisal?

It's the part of the appraisal where the appraiser estimates the property's market rent using comparable rentals. For one-unit properties it's usually Fannie Mae Form 1007, and for 2-4 units it's part of Form 1025.

### Does a DSCR lender use the lease or the appraiser's rent?

It varies. Many lenders use the lower of the lease or market rent. Some allow a higher lease with a signed lease and proof of payment. Confirm your lender's rule before you lock.

### What happens if the appraisal rent is too low for my DSCR?

You can ask your lender for a reconsideration with better comparable rentals, put more money down, look at an interest-only or buydown option, or choose a lender that allows a lower ratio.

### Can I get a DSCR loan on a vacant property?

Often, yes. For a vacant property or new purchase, lenders typically use the appraiser's market rent from Form 1007 or Form 1025.

### How is market rent calculated on Form 1007?

The appraiser compares the property with similar rentals, usually at least three, adjusts for significant differences such as size, condition, utilities, or furniture, and reconciles to a single monthly market rent.
