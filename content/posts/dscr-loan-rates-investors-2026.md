**DSCR loan rates don't have one published national average. They depend on your DSCR, credit, down payment, and prepayment terms. For context, Freddie Mac's 30-year conventional average was 7.32% on October 22, 2026. DSCR loans are non-QM investor loans and are usually priced differently than that benchmark.**

Here's what investors should know about rates, rents, and buying activity this fall, and what it means for your DSCR.

## Key Takeaways

- **Freddie Mac:** 30-year fixed averaged **7.32%** on Oct. 22, 2026, down from 7.40% a week earlier but up from **6.19%** a year ago.
- **Rents are mixed:** Zillow shows asking rents **up 2.7%** year over year. Realtor.com and Apartment List both show **down 0.4%**.
- **Investors are active:** NAR says investors and second-home buyers were **20%** of September sales, up from 15%.
- **Higher rates lower DSCR** on the same rent. Run the numbers before you offer.
- DSCR pricing **varies by lender**, which is why a broker can help.

## Where Are Mortgage Rates in Fall 2026?

- **Freddie Mac PMMS (Oct. 22, 2026):** 30-year fixed **7.32%**, down from 7.40% the week before. A year ago it was 6.19%. The 15-year averaged **6.68%** ([Freddie Mac](https://www.freddiemac.com/pmms)).
- **Mortgage News Daily:** Its daily index showed a top-tier 30-year fixed at about **7.30%** in its Oct. 22, 2026 update. MND said rates were holding near their lowest levels in nearly a month ([Mortgage News Daily](https://www.mortgagenewsdaily.com/mortgage-rates)).
- **September context:** Freddie Mac's 30-year average was 6.86% in September, up from 6.67% in August, as reported in [MND's existing home sales recap](https://www.mortgagenewsdaily.com/news/10162026-existing-home-sales-nar-inventory-prices-appr).

**Important:** These benchmarks track **owner-occupied** loans. They are not DSCR rates and not quotes from Coastal Funding. Use them to understand the direction of the market.

## How Are DSCR Loan Rates Set?

DSCR loans are **non-QM** (non-qualified mortgage) loans. Lenders price them using factors like:

- **DSCR ratio:** Higher ratios usually get better pricing.
- **Credit score:** Higher scores usually help.
- **Loan-to-value (LTV):** A bigger down payment often lowers the rate.
- **Prepayment penalty:** Many DSCR loans offer lower rates in exchange for a longer prepayment penalty.
- **Property type:** Single-family, 2-4 units, condos, and short-term rentals may price differently.
- **Loan purpose:** Cash-out refinances often price a bit higher than purchases.
- **Interest-only options:** Available on some programs, with different pricing.

Because these factors vary so much, two investors can get very different DSCR rates on the same day. These are general industry practices, not Coastal Funding program terms.

## What Is Happening With Rents?

Different reports measure rent in different ways, so they don't always agree:

- **Zillow (Sept. 2026):** The typical asking rent was **$1,932**, up **2.7%** from a year earlier. Zillow also said rental demand was up **19.2%** year over year ([Zillow Research](https://www.zillow.com/research/september-2026-rent-report/)).
- **Realtor.com (Sept. 2026):** Median asking rent for 0-2 bedroom units in the 50 largest metros was **$1,694**, down **0.4%**, the slowest decline in three years. About **43.9%** of listings offered concessions ([Realtor.com](https://www.realtor.com/research/september-2026-rent/)).
- **Apartment List (Sept. 2026):** National median rent was **$1,388**, down **0.4%** from a year ago, with multifamily vacancy falling to **7%** ([Apartment List](https://www.apartmentlist.com/research/national-rent-data)).

**What it means:** National rent declines are slowing, and some measures show growth again. But your DSCR depends on **your** market rent. Use local comps and the appraiser's rent schedule, not national headlines.

## Are Investors Still Buying?

Yes. The National Association of REALTORS® reported for September 2026 ([NAR](https://www.nar.realtor/newsroom/existing-home-sales-increased-1-5-in-september-according-to-nar-report)):

- Existing-home sales rose **1.5%** to a **3.99 million** annual pace.
- **Individual investors and second-home buyers** made up **20%** of sales, up from 15% a year earlier.
- **Cash sales** were **27%**.
- Inventory reached **1.60 million** homes, a **4.8-month** supply, and the median price was **$417,500**, up 1.3% year over year.

More inventory and longer days on market (33 days) can give investors more room to negotiate.

## How Do Higher Rates Change Your DSCR?

Here's a simple example using a $300,000 loan, $2,600 rent, and $450 a month for taxes and insurance. The two rates are Freddie Mac's averages from a year ago and today, used only to show the math, not DSCR rate quotes.

| | 6.19% rate | 7.32% rate |
|---|---|---|
| Principal & interest | $1,835 | $2,061 |
| Taxes + insurance | $450 | $450 |
| **PITIA** | **$2,285** | **$2,511** |
| **DSCR** ($2,600 rent) | **1.14** | **1.04** |

About $225 more a month cut the DSCR from 1.14 to 1.04. To get back above a higher ratio, an investor might put more down, negotiate a lower price, or look for a property with stronger rent. Learn the formula in [How to Calculate DSCR](/posts/how-to-calculate-dscr/).
