A rental offer can fall apart when the lender first asks for tax returns the investor was not planning to use, or when a 1031 clock is already running. This guide explains what investor agents typically need and how we work those files.

## Key Takeaways

- **DSCR loans qualify on rent ÷ PITIA**, which is often a better fit for investors than a conventional debt-to-income review.
- **Entity structure should be settled early.** Many DSCR programs allow an LLC, usually with a personal guarantee.
- **Rent documentation matters.** A lease or an appraiser's Form 1007 rent schedule can make or break the ratio.
- **1031 and year-end deadlines** leave less room for a late lender change.
- **Call (833) 457-6500** to introduce an investor or a listing that needs a DSCR preapproval.

## What Do Investor Agents Need From a DSCR Lender?

Agents who specialize in investment property usually need five practical things:

1. **A preapproval written for an investment property**, including the likely entity name if the LLC will be the borrower.
2. **A lender who understands DSCR math**, so a 2- to 4-unit building, a short-term rental, or a vacant property is not treated like a first-time buyer file.
3. **A plan for rent documentation**, whether that is a lease, market rent, or [Form 1007, the appraisal rent schedule](/posts/dscr-appraisal-rent-schedule-form-1007/).
4. **Honest updates** if the ratio comes in below the target and the loan amount or down payment has to change.
5. **Availability across time zones and after hours**, because investor offers and 1031 deadlines do not keep bankers' hours.

Coastal Funding is a mortgage broker, not a single-program shop. We compare DSCR guidelines so the agent is not sending the client to start over when one wholesale lender declines a property type. Our post on [how to choose a DSCR lender](/posts/best-dscr-lenders-how-to-choose/) explains the questions we ask of each program.

## Why Does Timing Matter More on Investor Files?

[Zillow](https://www.zillow.com/learn/how-long-does-it-take-to-close-on-a-house/), citing a 2026 ICE study, reports that the average purchase closing takes about 37 days, with conventional loans often closing 30 to 45 days after the contract is signed. DSCR files can follow a similar window, but they have extra moving parts: entity documents, rent schedules, and sometimes a 1031 exchange.

If the investor is in a 1031, identification and closing deadlines are statutory. Our [DSCR and 1031 year-end guide](/posts/dscr-loan-1031-exchange-year-end/) covers the loan side of that calendar. An agent should not introduce a new lender with two weeks left on an exchange if a DSCR preapproval could have been started earlier.

LLC documents also take time. Our [DSCR loan for an LLC](/posts/dscr-loan-llc/) post explains why the entity plan should be in place before the contract, not the week of closing.
