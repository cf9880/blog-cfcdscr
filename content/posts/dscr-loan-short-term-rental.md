**Yes, you can often use a DSCR loan for an Airbnb or other short-term rental. Instead of checking your personal income, the lender qualifies the loan on the property's short-term rental income compared with its monthly payment (PITIA). How that income is measured varies by lender, so the details matter.**

## Key Takeaways

- Many DSCR lenders allow **short-term rentals (STRs)** like Airbnb and VRBO properties.
- Income usually comes from **booking history, an appraiser's STR estimate, or a data report** such as AirDNA.
- STR loans may have **lower max LTV, higher DSCR minimums, or more reserves** than long-term rentals. It varies by lender.
- **Local STR rules and HOA bylaws** can make or break the deal.
- AirDNA reported U.S. STR occupancy of **53.6%** and an average daily rate of **$254** in September 2026.

## What Is a Short-Term Rental DSCR Loan?

A **short-term rental DSCR loan** is an investment-property mortgage for a home you rent by the night or week. It works like any [DSCR loan](/posts/how-to-calculate-dscr/):

**DSCR = monthly rental income ÷ monthly PITIA**

The difference is where the "rent" comes from. A long-term rental has a lease. A short-term rental has nightly bookings that rise and fall with the seasons.

## How Do Lenders Count Airbnb Income for DSCR?

Most lenders use one of these methods:

1. **Booking history.** If the property has been rented short-term, the lender may average 12 months of income from Airbnb, VRBO, or a property manager's statements.
2. **Appraiser's short-term rent estimate.** Some appraisers can provide a short-term rental analysis based on nearby STR comparables.
3. **Third-party data projection.** Some lenders accept a report from a data provider such as AirDNA that estimates yearly revenue.
4. **Long-term market rent.** Some lenders simply use long-term rent, even for an STR. That's more conservative and can lower your ratio.

Many lenders also **reduce** STR income by an expense or vacancy factor, or set a minimum DSCR that's higher than for long-term rentals. Ask early which method a lender uses. It can change the ratio a lot.

### Quick example

These numbers are made up to show the math.

- Projected yearly STR revenue: **$48,000** → $4,000 a month
- Lender expense factor of 20% (example only) → **$3,200** qualifying income
- PITIA: **$2,600**

$3,200 ÷ $2,600 = **1.23 DSCR**.

If that same lender used long-term rent of $2,300 instead, the ratio would be $2,300 ÷ $2,600 = **0.88**. Same house, very different result.

## What Are Typical STR DSCR Loan Requirements?

Every lender is different. Here's what investors commonly see in the market:

- **Down payment:** Often **20% to 25% or more**, and sometimes more for STRs than for long-term rentals
- **Credit score:** Higher scores usually unlock better pricing and leverage
- **Reserves:** Several months of PITIA in the bank is common
- **Property type:** Single-family homes, 2-4 units, and some condos. **Condotels** and rural properties may have extra rules
- **Ownership:** Many programs let you close in an **LLC**
- **Prepayment penalty:** Common on DSCR loans, often with options to shorten or buy it out

These are typical ranges, not Coastal Funding program terms.

## What Should You Check Before Buying an Airbnb?

Before you make an offer, check:

- **City and county STR rules.** Some places require permits, cap the number of STRs, or ban them in certain zones.
- **HOA rules.** Many HOAs restrict rentals under 30 days.
- **Insurance.** STRs may need a special policy. Get quotes early, because insurance hits your PITIA.
- **Seasonality.** Make sure the off-season months still work.
- **Furnishing costs.** Budget for furniture, linens, and setup. Lenders usually won't finance those.

## STR or Long-Term Rental: Which Is Better for DSCR?

A short-term rental can earn more gross income, which may raise your DSCR. But it also brings more work, more rules, and more swings. A long-term rental is steadier and easier to underwrite. Some investors buy an STR and keep a long-term rent "Plan B" that still covers the payment.
