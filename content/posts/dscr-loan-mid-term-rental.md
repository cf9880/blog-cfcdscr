**Yes, a DSCR loan can often finance a mid-term rental, meaning a furnished home rented for a month or more to guests like travel nurses, relocating workers or winter snowbirds. The lender qualifies the property on its rent compared with its monthly payment, not on your personal income. The key question is which rent figure the lender will use.**

Winter is when many investors start looking at furnished monthly rentals, especially in warm-weather markets that draw seasonal visitors. Before you buy, it helps to understand how DSCR lenders look at this type of income and where the risks are.

## Key Takeaways

- **Mid-term rentals** are typically furnished stays of about 28 to 30 days or longer.
- **DSCR = monthly rent ÷ monthly PITIA.** Many lenders look for 1.0 or higher, with stronger terms at higher ratios. Requirements vary by lender.
- **Rent used for qualifying varies.** Many lenders start with long-term market rent from the appraisal; some accept documented furnished income.
- **Demand is market-specific.** Airbnb reported that monthly stays grew more slowly than short-term stays in North America in Q3 2026.
- **Check local rules, insurance and HOA limits** before you furnish a property.

## What Is a Mid-Term Rental?

A **mid-term rental** sits between a nightly vacation rental and a 12-month lease. It is usually furnished and rented for one to several months. Airbnb, in its [Q3 2026 shareholder letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1559720/000119312526449727/d437988dex991.htm), defines **monthly stays** as trips of 28 days or more.

Common guests include:

- Traveling nurses and other healthcare workers on temporary assignments.
- Employees relocating for a new job.
- Families between homes during a renovation or insurance claim.
- **Snowbirds** spending part of the winter in a warmer place.

## How Do DSCR Loans Work for Mid-Term Rentals?

A **DSCR (debt service coverage ratio) loan** is a business-purpose investment-property loan that qualifies based on the property's cash flow. If you are new to the math, start with our guide on [how to calculate DSCR](/posts/how-to-calculate-dscr/).

The formula:

**DSCR = monthly rental income ÷ monthly PITIA** (principal, interest, taxes, insurance and association dues)

### Which rent does the lender use?

This is the biggest difference between mid-term and long-term rentals. Lenders take different approaches:

- **Long-term market rent.** Many lenders use the appraiser's rent schedule (often Fannie Mae Form 1007), which estimates unfurnished 12-month rent. Our [Form 1007 guide](/posts/dscr-appraisal-rent-schedule-form-1007/) explains how it works.
- **Documented furnished income.** Some lenders will consider a track record of furnished or short-term income, such as 12 months of booking statements or bank deposits.
- **Signed leases.** A lender may accept a current lease, sometimes with limits on how much of the higher furnished rent counts.

Because terms vary, run your numbers using long-term rent first. If the deal only works at the furnished rent, ask up front whether a lender will count it.

### A simple hypothetical example

*These figures are for illustration only, not a quote or a market estimate.*

- Monthly PITIA: $2,400
- Long-term market rent on the appraisal: $2,300 → DSCR about 0.96
- Documented furnished rent: $3,200 → DSCR about 1.33

The same property can look very different depending on which income the lender accepts. That is why lender selection matters.

## Is Mid-Term Rental Demand Strong Right Now?

It depends on the location. In its Q3 2026 letter, Airbnb said that in North America, "growth in short-term stays and entire homes, particularly listings with four or more bedrooms, continued to outpace monthly stays (trips of 28 days or more) and private rooms," a trend it had seen for over a year. That does not mean monthly rentals are weak everywhere, but it is a reminder to check local demand rather than assume it.

Good local signals include major hospitals, military bases, large employers that relocate staff, and winter-visitor demand in warm-weather towns.

## How Do Today's Rates Affect the Math?

Higher rates raise PITIA, which lowers DSCR. Mortgage News Daily's daily index showed **7.51%** for a top-tier 30-year fixed conventional scenario on November 16, 2026, and Freddie Mac's weekly average was **7.11%** on November 12, 2026. DSCR loans are priced differently from those conventional benchmarks, so treat them only as market context. Options like an [interest-only DSCR loan](/posts/interest-only-dscr-loan/) can lower the early payment, though they have trade-offs.

## What Should You Check Before Buying a Mid-Term Rental?

1. **Local rules.** Some cities and HOAs set minimum stay lengths or restrict rentals. Rules for stays of 30 days or more can differ from nightly rentals.
2. **Insurance.** Furnished rentals may need a different policy than a standard landlord policy.
3. **Furnishing costs.** Budget for furniture, linens, internet and utilities, which you usually pay.
4. **Vacancy between guests.** Monthly stays can leave gaps between tenants.
5. **Reserves.** Many DSCR lenders require months of payments in reserve.

### What is a mid-term rental?

### Can I use a DSCR loan for a mid-term rental?

Often, yes. DSCR loans qualify the property on its rental income instead of your personal income. How a lender counts mid-term income varies; many start from long-term market rent on the appraisal, and some accept documented furnished-rental history.

### How is DSCR calculated?

DSCR equals the property's monthly rental income divided by its monthly housing payment: principal, interest, taxes, insurance and any HOA dues. A DSCR of 1.0 means rent exactly covers the payment.
